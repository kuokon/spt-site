SPT - Smart Similar Photo Cleaner

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

SPT ("the app") is a Windows desktop application that helps you find similar photos, choose your best shot, and delete duplicates to reclaim disk space. This policy explains what information we collect and how we handle it.

The short version: Your photos, file names and folder paths never leave your device. All scanning and analysis happens locally. Optional, anonymous crash and usage statistics are collected only if you consent, and you can turn them off any time.

1. Photos and files you scan

All photo scanning, grouping, quality scoring and deletion happens entirely on your computer. SPT does not upload, transmit or store any of your photos, thumbnails, file names, folder paths, or EXIF metadata. The app does not require an account.

2. Optional anonymous diagnostics (with your consent)

On first launch, SPT asks whether you allow anonymous diagnostics and usage statistics. If you allow:

  • Crash reports — technical crash data (e.g. error type, app version, OS version) is sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs.
  • Usage statistics — anonymized events such as "scan started" or "group reviewed" are sent to PostHog to help us improve the app. No photo content or file paths are included.

Your device is identified only by a randomly generated, local ID. We cannot link events to you personally. If you do not consent, no diagnostics are collected at all.

3. Feedback you send

If you use the in-app "Send Feedback" form and choose to include your email address, we receive that message and email so we can reply. We use it only to respond to you and improve the app. You can also contact us directly at jack.koren@proton.me.

4. Purchases

SPT is distributed through the Microsoft Store. Purchases, payments, refunds and license management are handled entirely by Microsoft through your Microsoft account. We never receive or store your payment card details. We only see whether a license is active.

5. How to opt out

You can turn diagnostics on or off at any time in the app: Settings → Diagnostics. Turning them off stops all future data collection. Already-collected data can be deleted by contacting us at jack.koren@proton.me.

6. Third-party services

  • Sentry — crash reporting (only with consent).
  • PostHog — product analytics (only with consent).
  • Microsoft Store — distribution, purchases and licenses.
  • Email (your device\'s mail app, mailto fallback) — forwards your in-app feedback messages to us.

Each service processes only the data described above and under its own privacy policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

Anonymous diagnostic events are retained for up to 13 months by our analytics providers. Feedback messages are kept until the related issue is resolved, and can be deleted on request. To request deletion, email jack.koren@proton.me.

8. Security

We use reputable providers with industry-standard security. Because almost all data stays on your device, the data we do receive is minimal and anonymized.

9. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify users in the app before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email jack.koren@proton.me. We respond to every message.
